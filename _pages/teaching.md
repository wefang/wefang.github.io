- **JHSPH 140.776** Statistical Computing · Fall 2017, Fall 2018

**For public health and laboratory scientists**

- **JHSPH 140.621–624** Statistical Methods in Public Health · Fall 2016, Spring 2017, Fall 2017, Spring 2018, Spring 2020, Fall 2020, Spring 2021
- **JHSPH 140.615** Statistics for Laboratory Scientists · Spring 2016, Spring 2019. Wrote problems and laboratory exercises with the course director.
- **AS.280.345** Public Health Biostatistics · Fall 2015, Fall 2019
