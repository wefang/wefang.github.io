I taught in fourteen course-terms across five courses, to two audiences, graduate students in biostatistics and related quantitative fields, and scientists and public health students meeting statistical inference for the first time.

**For graduate students in biostatistics and related quantitative fields**

- **JHSPH 140.688** [Statistics for Genomics](https://publichealth.jhu.edu/course/28956) · Spring 2020
- **JHSPH 140.776** [Statistical Computing](https://publichealth.jhu.edu/course/27851) · Fall 2017, Fall 2018

**For public health and laboratory scientists**

- **JHSPH 140.621–624** [Statistical Methods in Public Health](https://e-catalogue.jhu.edu/course-descriptions/biostatistics/) · Fall 2016, Spring 2017, Fall 2017, Spring 2018, Spring 2020, Fall 2020, Spring 2021
- **JHSPH 140.615** [Statistics for Laboratory Scientists](https://www.biostat.jhsph.edu/~iruczins/teaching/140.615/) · Spring 2016, Spring 2019. Wrote problems and laboratory exercises with the course director.
- **AS.280.345** [Public Health Biostatistics](https://e-catalogue.jhu.edu/course-descriptions/public_health_studies/) · Fall 2015, Fall 2019
